How a conversation goes

No method. Just two people, paying attention.

Arrive

Come with an idea you can't stop turning over, a question you're stuck on, or nothing in particular. There's no right way to start.

Question

"Is that true? Who told you that?"

We look gently at the beliefs and limits that feel like facts, and see which ones hold up when someone asks.

Listen

When the noise settles, what's left is your own voice. I don't hand you answers. I keep you company while you hear yours.

Meet Jesse

I'm not a guru. I'm a good listener.

I'm Jesse O'Meara. Somewhere along the way I got curious about the stories running my own life: the quiet "I can't" and "that's just how I am" that sound like facts until someone asks about them.

What helped me most wasn't advice. It was a person who would sit with me, ask honest questions, and wait. Watchful is me offering the same. If you want to think out loud, test an idea, or find your way back to what you actually feel, my door is open.
