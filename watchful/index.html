No method. Just two people, paying attention.

Meet Jesse

We explore together. One awareness, many paths.

Hi, I'm Jesse. Welcome. Somewhere along the way I got curious about the stories running my own life: the quiet "I can't" and "that's just how I am" that sound like facts until someone asks about them.

What can one person really share that helps another? Perhaps that includes what's honest, what has integrity, what seems to work. We watch, observe and inquire together, one consciousness looking at itself from two different paths.

What helped me most wasn't advice. It was sitting with someone, talking honestly, and finding that we both came away understanding more than we started with. The Sufis call this sohbet: companionship in conversation, where two people grow together.

I try not to take any of this too seriously, myself included. I don't always manage it. I'm fascinated by consciousness, imagination and how an ordinary moment contains the miraculous.

That's what Watchful is. If you want to think out loud, test an idea, or find your way back to what you actually feel, the door is open.
